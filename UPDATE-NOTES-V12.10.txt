DivyaLok Bhakti LP V12.10 review strip reliability fix

Review strip fixes:
- fixed the auto-scroll implementation so it moves reliably even on browsers that integer-round scrollLeft values
- mobile auto-scroll remains deliberately slow at 5 px/sec; desktop at 7 px/sec
- manual horizontal drag/swipe still works
- long reviews now open in a full-width natural-height pop-out below the strip, with no internal review scrollbar
- page can still scroll vertically while a review is open
- strip can still be manually scrolled horizontally while a review is open
- auto movement pauses while the full review is open and resumes after it closes
- the pop-out auto-closes when its source review is moved out of the visible strip or when the expanded review is scrolled completely past the viewport
- Collapse button closes the full review immediately
- Read more remains hidden for reviews that fit within five lines

Browser harness validation:
- mobile 390px viewport: strip moved 16px over ~3.2 sec, matching the intended ~5 px/sec rate
- long review pop-out rendered at natural height with no internal scrollbar
- automatic movement paused at 0px delta while pop-out was open
- automatic movement resumed after collapse
- manually moving the source card out of the strip closed the pop-out automatically
