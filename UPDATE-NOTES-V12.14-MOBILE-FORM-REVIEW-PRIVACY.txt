V12.14 targeted fixes

1. Mobile form jump fix
- Sticky Review control changed from an anchor to a button, removing native #testimonials navigation/restoration.
- Review shortcut is guarded during active form entry and for a short grace period after keyboard/focus transitions.
- Sticky buttons remain visible while form fields are being edited.
- Existing payment, pricing, review strip, and page layout logic otherwise unchanged.

2. Review privacy checkbox semantics
- Checkbox now means: Hide my review from the website.
- Unchecked = public display allowed after manual approval.
- Checked = private review.
- Backend still receives the existing publicConsent boolean, inverted from the new hide checkbox, so moderation/public-review functions remain compatible.
