V11 WHITE MARBLE / BOTTLE GREEN / REVIEWS UPDATE

Visual
- Changed page base from warm off-white/ivory to true white marble.
- Added visible soft grey marble veining across desktop and mobile.
- Kept gold accents and bottle-green primary actions.
- Forced white text on all bottle-green CTA buttons, including nested spans/icons and high-specificity testimonial/payment/sticky CTAs.

Reviews
- Public review API now returns the stored full name when available.
- New review consent copy states that full name, city, stars and feedback may be shown publicly after approval.
- Legacy records for which only a first name/Anonymous exists are not fabricated.
- Review auto-scroll speed reduced to 6px/s mobile and 10px/s desktop.
- Mobile review cards are compact squares with two visible per screen.
- Tablet 641-900px also uses a two-card layout.
- Long reviews use Read more inside the square card and become internally scrollable.
- Automatic review movement pauses while a review/reply is expanded.
- Manual horizontal strip scrolling remains available while expanded; vertical page/content scrolling remains available.
- Expanded review/reply auto-collapses after its card leaves the viewport.
